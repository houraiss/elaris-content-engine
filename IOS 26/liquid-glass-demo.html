Faking Liquid Glass with CSS

Background is a colorful gradient, not solid white — the point is the glass reads correctly against anything behind it.

1. Base frosted glass

backdrop-filter: blur() saturate() + a semi-transparent white fill + a brighter top/left border to fake a light source, plus a soft drop shadow for lift.

2. Motion-reactive highlight

Move your cursor over this card. The highlight is a radial-gradient pinned to pointer position — on a real device you'd drive --x/--y from the gyroscope instead of the mouse.

3. Actual lensing (SVG displacement)

This layers an feDisplacementMap filter into backdrop-filter so the background genuinely warps, not just blurs. Chromium browsers render true distortion; Safari/Firefox silently fall back to plain blur.
